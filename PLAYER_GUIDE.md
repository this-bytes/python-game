# 🎮 TRUE IDLE GAME - PLAYER GUIDE

## Welcome to Your Cybersecurity Firm!

This is now a **TRUE IDLE GAME** with RPG/RTS elements. Your firm runs automatically, but you can intervene strategically for bonus rewards!

---

## 🤖 Auto-Play: The Core Mechanic

### How It Works
- **Specialists automatically assign themselves** to incoming incidents
- **No clicking required** - your firm runs on autopilot
- **Close the game** - it continues progressing offline
- **Reopen later** - collect all the money and XP you earned!

### What You See
```
Top-right corner:
┌─────────────────────────┐
│ ✓ AUTO-PLAY ACTIVE      │
│ Threshold: Difficulty ≤3│
│ (Press A to toggle)     │
└─────────────────────────┘
```

**Green = AUTO-PLAY ON** (default)  
**Red = AUTO-PLAY OFF** (manual mode)

## ⚡ Synergy System: Strategic Depth

### What Are Synergies?
Each specialist has **2 special threat types** they're experts at. When they handle matching incidents, they get **MASSIVE BONUSES**:

- 🔥 **1.5x - 2.5x XP**
- 💰 **1.2x - 1.8x Money**
- ⚡ **1.3x - 1.8x Speed**
- ✅ **+10% - +20% Success Rate**

### Example Specialist
```
Alice Chen (Network Security) - Level 5
Synergies:
  🎯 DDoS Attack: 2.5x XP, 1.8x $, 1.8x speed
  🎯 SQL Injection: 1.5x XP, 1.5x $, 1.3x speed
```

When Alice handles a DDoS Attack, she earns **2.5x XP** and **1.8x money**!

## 🎮 How To Play

### Idle Mode (Default)
1. **Start the game**
2. Watch incidents appear and auto-assign
3. Check synergy panel (Press S) occasionally
4. Manually assign high-synergy incidents for bonuses
5. **Close the game** whenever you want
6. Come back later for offline rewards!

### Active Mode
1. Press **A** to toggle auto-play OFF
2. Manually assign all incidents yourself
3. Use synergy panel to find best matches
4. Press **A** again to re-enable auto-play

### Recommended Strategy
- **Leave auto-play ON** for most incidents
- **Press S** to check synergy opportunities every minute
- **Manually assign** when you see 🔥 URGENT synergies
- Let everything else auto-assign

## 🌙 Offline Progression

### How It Works
When you close the game:
1. Game simulates up to **24 hours** of progress
2. Calculates incidents generated

4. Awards money and XP
