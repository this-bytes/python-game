## 🔥 WHAT TO WATCH FOR

### **Combo Counter (Top-Right)**
- Current combo streak
- Current multiplier
- Changes color as it grows
- **PULSES** when you add to combo

### **Risk Contract Notifications (Top-Left)**
- Shows when risk contracts are offered
- Icon indicates contract type
- Description shows multiplier/penalty
- **GOLD BORDER** on incident card in queue

### **Floating Reward Text (Center)**
- Shows exact money/XP gained
- Includes multiplier info
- Color indicates reward tier

### **Particle Effects (Everywhere!)**

- **MASSIVE STORMS** on epic rewards
